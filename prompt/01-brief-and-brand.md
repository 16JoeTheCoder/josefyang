# 01. Brief and brand

## Goal

josefyang.com is Josef Yang's personal brand home: a portfolio of his engineering builds (hardware and software) and his progress along the way. The same identity will later carry into his CV, Instagram posts, presentations and video graphics, so the system must be durable. All of his builds live on Cloudflare under josefyang.com, with live apps on subdomains (e.g. `fuse.josefyang.com`).

## Audience

1. University admissions officers (primary). He is aiming for an engineering degree abroad.
2. Internship recruiters.
3. Curious teachers and professors.

Test every decision: would all three read it as modern, professional and trustworthy, and remember it?

## Brand principles

- The brand is **Josef as a person**, not a topic. Never theme the identity around renewable energy or any single field.
- Must not look AI-made. Must stand out by doing what's rare, not what's trending.
- One idea per section, one main focus.
- Japanese restraint (empty space, discipline) with bold Swiss typography.
- Contrast between pages (each page has its own ground), but the same strict grid on every page.
- Honest over impressive: no padded stats, no invented accolades.
- Personality through small real details (a cappuccino, a phone playing jazz, a watch on the floor), never through gimmicky decoration.

## Tone of voice

Short, plain, confident, first person ("I build…"). No hype, no exclamation marks, no corporate filler ("leverage", "seamless", "passionate"). Sentence case everywhere. Buttons say exactly what happens ("View project", "Open Fuse", "View the devlog").
