A bit about me, off the record

I learn by breaking things

Most of what I know came from something not working the first time. The failures page is there on purpose.

I build my own systems

I plan my days in an app I made and keep my notes in another. If a tool doesn't fit how I think, I build one.

I like problems I can predict

Big physical systems you can model and check. That's the part of physics that pulls me in.

Jazz on, after dark

Not for the instruments. For the calm. Most builds happen late with a record going.

Kopi first

Kopi susu in the day, espresso when a build runs late. There's a cup in almost every photo.

Watches, and the pitch

I love small machines that must be right every second. And I captain the school football team.
